Start at the front desk

The ground floor has the reception and office. The front desk opens at 9 am, and 24×7 security and CCTV cover the building around the clock.

Rooms, kitchen and a foosball table

Furnished rooms open off a bright corridor. Sharing rooms have a common kitchen, each bathroom has a geyser, and the recreation area has a foosball table for the evenings.

Then go all the way up

The terrace is on top. It looks out over the trees, and it is the best place to be when the sun goes down.

Is there a curfew?

No. The residency has no timing restrictions, and 24×7 security and CCTV cover the building.

Is food included?

No. Tiffin service is optional at ₹50,000 a year and is not part of the rent.

What does the rent cover?

Electricity, Wi-Fi, cleaning, RO drinking water and 24×7 security are included in every option. Single and double sharing also include the common kitchen. The flat has its own kitchen and living area.

Can four friends take one place?

Yes. The 2BHK flat is for four students at ₹50,000 a month, which is ₹12,500 each if you split it evenly.

Is there a lift?

Yes. The building has an elevator, and the terrace is on top.

How far is the campus?

Within walking distance of UPES Kandoli campus. Call us and we will tell you the way.
